CHANGES
Anatomical meshes and curves were extracted and evaluated with Blender 5.2.1.
Annotation/helper objects and segmentation overlays were excluded. Meshes were
grouped into selectable systems, some surfaces subdivided and skin boundaries
welded. New digital-atlas materials were authored. GLB exported for browser use.
Anatomical mesh derivatives retain the applicable source licenses and attribution.
The interactive day and organ animations are educational illustrative additions,
not measured patient data or physiologically validated simulation.
